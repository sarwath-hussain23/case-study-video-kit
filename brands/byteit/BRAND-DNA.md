Royal blue (#0530C0) is the most used brand color on the site: links, borders and full-width bands. The main call-to-action button ("Schedule a Free Consultation") uses a darker red-orange, #C5310D. The video keeps #F5831F as the accent and #E02B1F for alert states; use #C5310D if an on-screen button is shown.

## Typography

- Family: **Poppins** (Google Fonts), weights 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800
- Site: H1 48 px / 500, H2 30 px / 600, body 18 px / 300 with 1.6 line height, nav 17 px / 600
- Video: headings scale up to 62-88 px / 700 for 1080p legibility; body copy stays light (300)

## Visual motifs

Background motif in the video: **arc** (arc, dots, bars or none).

- Orange hand-drawn swoosh under key heading words
- Large light-gray circular arc behind hero content
- Full-width royal-blue band sections
- Pill-shaped buttons (30-50 px radius)
- White cards with soft navy shadows
- Orange and red dots from the logo

## Personality and tone of voice

A reassuring, plain-spoken IT partner for small and mid-size businesses. Leads with trust and time given back, not with jargon or fear.

- Trusted
- Practical
- Approachable
- Growth-focused

**Headlines from the brand's own site:**

- Your Trusted IT Provider
- Serving Businesses of All Sizes
- Streamlining Your IT, Enabling Rapid & Scalable Business Growth
- Don't Let IT Challenges Hold You Back!
- Ready To Transform Your IT?

## Do / don't

- Do lead with the business outcome: time back, growth, peace of mind
- Do talk to the viewer as "you" and "your business"
- Do use concrete numbers from the source document
- Don't use fear-heavy security language
- Don't put more than two accent colors in one scene
- Don't stretch, recolor or crop the logo
